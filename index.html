C++ Console Application

Console Stock Car Racing

Console Stock Car Racing is a C++ game where players make pit strategy decisions during a five lap race against three AI drivers. Players enter their name, choose a car number, and check their lap times, fuel, tire condition, and position after each lap. Between laps, they can stay on track with worn tires or spend 15 seconds in the pits for fuel and fresh tires. The driver with the lowest total race time wins.

Key Features

  • One major feature is the pit stop system. Between laps, players can check their fuel level and tire condition, then choose whether to stay on track or pit. A pit stop refills the tank and restores the tires, but it adds 15 seconds to the player’s race time. Since worn tires slow each lap, players have to decide whether fresh tires are worth the time lost in the pits.
  • Another major feature is the AI opponent system. Each race includes three opponents with randomly selected names and unique car numbers. Their lap times vary, and each opponent makes one pit stop on a randomly assigned lap. Players can see how those stops affect their position in the standings after each lap.
  • I built a menu that lets players start a race, read about the game, or exit. The game checks that the driver’s name isn’t blank and that the car number is a whole number from 1 to 99. If a player enters 12abc or 100, the game explains what to fix and prompts them again.

What I Learned

One challenge I faced was figuring out why a pit stop made a single lap appear to take over a minute. I traced the timing calculation and found that the lap summary was displaying the driver’s total race time. I fixed it by tracking the current lap separately and showing the driving time, the 15 second pit penalty, and the overall race time. I learned to check which value my code is displaying before changing the calculation itself.
